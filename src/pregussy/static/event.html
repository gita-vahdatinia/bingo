Pregussy Bingo

How to play

  1. You get your own board — nobody else has the same layout.
  2. Find someone the square is true for. Actually go ask them.
  3. Tap the square and put their name in.
  4. A full row, column, or diagonal is a bingo.

Tap a square, find someone it's true for, add their name. Tap again to change it.

BINGO!
